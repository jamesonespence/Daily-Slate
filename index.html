v2_2 - Nov 4, 2026

  • Fixed TV: Prime Video games were mislabeled (NBA Prime shown as ABC, NBA TV shown as Prime). Tonight's Senators at Sabres now shows Prime Video (Canada).

v2_1 - Nov 4, 2026

  • Built-in schedule is refreshed by hand when needed; the live feed handles day-to-day changes.
  • Full-season schedules: NBA to Apr 11, NHL to Apr 10, NFL to Jan 10, MLS through the playoffs. MLB is shown as offseason.
  • Toronto teams (Raptors, Maple Leafs, Toronto FC) always show first, plus a next-game panel.
  • Filters: league, date strip, quick ranges, team, network, search, Toronto only, favorites, national TV only.
  • Self-updating: the page checks a public sports feed in your browser for the latest times, venues, TV channels, odds and live scores, on load and every 5 minutes. No Claude needed.
  • The last-updated time is shown at the top, with a Live feed status and Refresh now button.
  • Days load 14 at a time; use Show everything for the full list.
  • Playoffs are added about a week before each round starts.

v2_0 - Nov 4, 2026

  • Full-season schedules, Toronto-first sorting, filters and the full-season date strip.
